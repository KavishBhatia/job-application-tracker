# Product

## Register

product

## Users

A single person (the developer) tracking their own job applications: company, role, status, interview rounds, and feedback. Used casually, at a desk, in whatever ambient light and whichever theme (light/dark) matches their system or mood at the time. No other users, no teams, no permissions model.

## Product Purpose

A lightweight personal job-application tracker: log applications via free text, track status and interview progress, export/import via CSV/Excel. Success is simply "the tool is pleasant enough to keep using and doesn't get in the way."

## Brand Personality

Warm, confident, and a little rich rather than flat or clinical. Not sterile "engineering tool gray." Color should feel intentional and varied (a full palette of distinct roles: accent, secondary accent, and vivid-but-legible status colors) rather than a single muted accent doing all the work.

## Anti-references

Flat, monochromatic "SaaS admin panel gray" dark mode (e.g. a single desaturated gray scale with one timid accent color). Overly cool/clinical tech-tool palettes. Anything that reads as lifeless or generic.

## Design Principles

- Warm neutrals over cool/clinical ones — every gray is tinted, never a flat desaturated gray.
- Color carries meaning and personality, not just decoration — status colors, accents, and headings each get a distinct, deliberate role.
- Legibility first: richer color must not come at the cost of WCAG AA contrast for text.
- Consistency across light and dark themes — both should feel like the same product, tuned for their respective viewing conditions, not two unrelated designs.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA text contrast. No color-blindness-specific requirements, but status is already conveyed via text label (not color alone) in the status dropdown, which should be preserved.
